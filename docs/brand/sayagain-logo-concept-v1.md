# SayAgain logo concept v1

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. Raster concept; not yet applied to the desktop app.

## Prompt

A professional minimalist logo on a SOLID OPAQUE WHITE background. White canvas, black ink. Brand name "SayAgain" in large perfectly readable black modern sans serif lettering. To the left, an original black symbol made from two curved speech bubbles forming the letter S, conveying conversation and echo. Small "HOTPOOR" beneath the wordmark. One large centered horizontal logo only. Clean flat graphic design, bold simple silhouette, generous whitespace, precisely balanced shapes. This is a language-learning app that helps people improve their real conversations and practice in their own voice. Black and white, warm and human, highly legible. No transparency. No shadows. No gradients. No glow. No mockups. NO BLACK BACKGROUND.
