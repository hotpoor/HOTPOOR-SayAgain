## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. A premium minimalist horizontal logo for the desktop language-learning app SayAgain, on a SOLID OPAQUE PURE WHITE background. To the left: a bold black S-shaped emblem built from two softly rounded speech bubbles, upper bubble pointing lower-right, lower bubble pointing lower-left, each with three small white circular speaking dots; sculpted negative space between them creates a clear flowing S. To the right: the exact text "SayAgain" in large bold friendly geometric sans serif black lettering, carefully kerned. Below the wordmark, centered under the WORDMARK ONLY, one discreet small regular-weight black line reading EXACTLY "by HOTPOOR XIALIWEI". The credit is approximately one eighth of the height of the SayAgain capital letters, modest letter spacing, with comfortable separation from the main wordmark. Product name is the strongest element, author credit secondary. Perfect flat clean graphic design, abundant white margins, excellent typographic hierarchy. Single logo composition only, no application mockups, no additional text, no gradients, no shadows, no lighting effects, no texture, no transparency. WHITE background, black logo and lettering. Landscape image.
